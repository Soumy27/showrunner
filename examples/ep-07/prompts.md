# ep-07 — shot prompts

Episode: "The Delay"
Written by tinyhorror-writer, prompts by tinyhorror-designer.

This example carries two deliberate drifts so a run of the checker shows a
failure as well as a row of passes. Shot 2 paraphrases canon instead of
restating it, which is the single most common way a series loses its own
character.

## Shot 1 — hook (3s)

> Mira, shaved head, red flight jacket, standing in the doorway of one
> apartment, flat, no lens flare. She is looking at the hallway light, which
> has not come on yet. Static wide. 9:16.

## Shot 2 — (6s)

> Close on a woman with short dark hair in a red flight jacket, breathing
> shallowly, flat, no lens flare. She counts under her breath.

## Shot 3 — (8s)

> Mira, shaved head, red flight jacket, turning away from the hallway into
> one apartment. The light comes on behind her, two seconds late, flat, no
> lens flare.

## Shot 4 — (7s)

> Mira, shaved head, red flight jacket, talking past the dark shape in the
> corner of one apartment, not to it, flat, no lens flare.

## Shot 5 — turn (6s)

> The hallway light goes off. Mira, shaved head, red flight jacket, does not
> turn around. One apartment, flat, no lens flare.

## Thumbnail prompts

1. Mira, shaved head, red flight jacket, half-lit in the doorway of one
   apartment, flat, no lens flare. Her face fills the left third.
2. The hallway light, off, with Mira's shaved head silhouetted against it,
   one apartment, flat, no lens flare.
3. Close on Mira's face, shaved head, looking past camera, red flight
   jacket, one apartment, flat, no lens flare.
