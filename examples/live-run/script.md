# Episode 1: Two Seconds

**Total: 30s** (3 + 4 + 3 + 5 + 3 + 4 + 4 + 4)

Cold-audience pattern: none yet. Written without it.

**Canon in play:** Mira. Shaved head. Red flight jacket. One apartment. The camera never leaves the room she is in. Lighting: flat, no lens flare.

**The idea, sharpened:** A late light is a faulty sensor. So Mira turns the switch off and the light still comes on, which means it was never late for her. Then it happens in the room she has shut herself into.

## Shots

**1. Hook (3s, 0:00-0:03).** Hallway, seen down its length, ceiling light off. Mira walks toward camera and passes under the light at 0:00.8. She keeps coming, looking ahead. At 0:02.8 the light clicks on behind her. She has not noticed.
Dialogue: none. Sound: the click.

**2. (4s, 0:03-0:07).** Closer on Mira, stopped beside the wall switch. She looks back over her shoulder at the light. She looks at it long enough for us to see she has noticed this before.
Dialogue: none.

**3. (3s, 0:07-0:10).** Close on the wall switch, her red sleeve in frame. Her finger pushes it down. The light goes off. Her finger stays on the switch a moment, then lifts.
Dialogue: none. Sound: the switch.

**4. (5s, 0:10-0:15).** From beside the switch, looking down the hallway. Mira walks away from camera and passes under the dark light at 0:11. She stops just beyond it, back to us, and does not turn.
Mira, at 0:12: "One."
Mira, at 0:13: "Two."
On "Two" the light clicks on above the empty stretch of floor behind her. Hold.

**5. (3s, 0:15-0:18).** The switch sharp in the foreground, still down. Beyond it, small and down the hallway, Mira has turned and is looking back at it, the lit light between them.
Dialogue: none.

**6. (4s, 0:18-0:22).** Mira, medium close, facing camera. Her eyes drop to the floor under the light, then move slowly along it toward her.
Mira, quietly: "It's not coming on for me."
She takes one step backward.

**7. (4s, 0:22-0:26).** Hard cut from the hallway. The first frame is the bedroom with Mira already inside it, both hands on the door, pushing it shut. It shuts. Eyes on the door, she backs across the room and passes a floor lamp, off, at 0:25.5. The lamp ends up between her and the door.
Dialogue: none. Sound: the door.

**8. (4s, 0:26-0:30).** Same room, wider. Mira foreground, lamp behind and beside her, shut door beyond. She watches the door.
Mira, at 0:26.5: "One."
She does not say two. At 0:27.5 the lamp clicks on, inside the room, with the door still shut. She does not turn her head. Only her eyes move toward it. Hold to 0:30, hard cut to black.

## Timing

The two-second gap is exact every time, and shots 1, 4 and 8 depend on it:

- Shot 1: passes 0:00.8, click 0:02.8
- Shot 4: passes 0:11, click 0:13
- Shot 8: passes 0:25.5, click 0:27.5

## Not yet in canon (for continuity to rule on, not to be assumed)

1. **The hallway counts as a room she is in.** The series rule needs this for the idea to work at all. The camera is in the hallway only while she is.
2. **The camera may go with her from one room to another,** as a hard cut with her already inside (shot 7). It never shows the hallway once she has left it.
3. **Apartment layout:** a hallway with one ceiling light and one wall switch, and a bedroom door at the end of it. The bedroom has a floor lamp.
4. **The two-second rule:** lights come on two seconds after Mira passes, whatever the switch says, and this is not limited to the hallway.
5. **Something may be following her.** The episode implies it and never shows or names it. Nothing about what it is, or whether it exists, should be recorded as fact.
6. **Mira speaks.** Canon sets voice.id but does not say it is hers. This script assumes it is.
