# Two Dots, episode 1: Forty Seconds

Total: 30s (3 + 4 + 5 + 3 + 4 + 4 + 3 + 4)

Cold-audience pattern: none yet. Written without it.

Story rule: the setting is a real-looking night city with blocks, a river and
curved streets, filling the frame and whole in frame for all 30 seconds, with no
camera moves. The river divides the city, west bank for orange and east bank for
blue. Above its bend the two trails share streets, on opposite sides. They never
touch until shot 7, when they meet on the only bridge. By shot 4 they have lit
every street except that bridge. On-screen words are large warm lowercase
captions.

## Shots

### 1. Hook (0:00-0:03, 3s)
On screen: `40 seconds apart. again.`
What moves: the orange dot glides out of a corner on a curved street, drawing its glowing trail. Once it has gone, the blue dot glides into the same corner on the far side of the street. Two short trails, a sliver apart.
Diagram: the night city.
Voice: Forty seconds apart. Again.

### 2. (0:03-0:07, 4s)
On screen: `same streets. every day.`
What moves: the same crossing repeats along three more curved streets, orange first, blue just behind. The two trails lengthen side by side.
Diagram: the night city, trails growing.
Voice: Same streets. Every day. Never the same moment.

### 3. (0:07-0:12, 5s)
On screen: counter, `year 1` rising to `year 7`
What moves: both dots glide faster. Orange trails branch out across the west bank and blue across the east, winding between the blocks and along the river, never touching.
Diagram: the night city with a year counter.
Voice: For seven years, their glowing lines filled the city.

### 4. (0:12-0:15, 3s)
On screen: `every street but one.`
What moves: the dots stop gliding. One street near the middle, the only bridge between the two banks, stays unlit, the only dark gap left between the orange and blue trails. A dashed street-grey tram line runs past each end of it.
Diagram: the whole city under both trails, one gap.
Voice: Every street but one.

### 5. Turn (0:15-0:19, 4s)
On screen: `then the last tram never came.`
What moves: the orange dot waits at one end of the dark street and the blue dot at the other. The dashed tram line fades slowly away. Nothing arrives.
Diagram: the night city, tram line fading.
Voice: Then one night, the last tram never came.

### 6. (0:19-0:23, 4s)
On screen: `so they walked.`
What moves: both dots turn into the dark street from opposite ends and glide slowly toward each other. Each trail lights the street behind it. The gap shrinks.
Diagram: the night city, the last gap closing.
Voice: So they walked.
(Silent after those three words, for the rest of the shot.)

### 7. Meeting (0:23-0:26, 3s)
On screen: `0 seconds apart.`
What moves: the dots stop, touching, and the two trails meet for the first time. Warm gold light spreads across the whole city from where they meet, block by block, over the streets and across the river.
Diagram: the night city, filling with warm gold light.
Voice: Zero seconds apart.

### 8. Ending (0:26-0:30, 4s)
On screen: `they never took the tram again.`
What moves: the warm gold light finishes reaching the edges of the city and stays. The two dots glide off together, side by side, drawing two trails close together down a curved street. Hold on the gold-lit city through the last second.
Diagram: the gold-lit city, no tram line.
Voice: They never took the tram again.

## Voice, in full (45 words)

Forty seconds apart. Again. Same streets. Every day. Never the same moment. For
seven years, their glowing lines filled the city. Every street but one. Then one
night, the last tram never came. So they walked. Zero seconds apart. They never
took the tram again.

Fiction: this story, its city and its two dots are invented.
