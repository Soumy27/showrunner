# Episode 1: Whatever the Switch Says

**Total: 30s** (3 + 4 + 5 + 3 + 5 + 4 + 3 + 3)

Cold-audience pattern: none yet. Written without it.

**Canon in play:** Mira. Shaved head. Red flight jacket. One apartment. The camera never leaves the room she is in. Lighting: flat, no lens flare.

**The idea, sharpened:** A switch that fails is only a faulty switch, so Mira has already cut hers out of the wall, and the light still comes on. She already knows it comes on two seconds after she passes, and she counts it out for us. So she stands still beneath it to beat it, and it comes on anyway.

**Location:** one room, the living room, for all 30 seconds. No window in shot.

## Shots

**1. Hook (3s, 0:00-0:03).** Looking down the length of the room, ceiling light off. The first frame has Mira mid-stride, directly under the light, walking toward camera. In her right hand, held at chest height, is a wall switch on its faceplate, two cut wires hanging from the back. She passes the light at 0:00.5 and keeps coming. At 0:02.5 the light clicks on behind her. She does not look back.
Dialogue: none. Sound: the click.

**2. (4s, 0:03-0:07).** The wall by the door, a square hole where the switch was, the wire ends capped inside it. Mira comes into frame and sets the switch down on the shelf beside the hole. She turns her head and looks back at the lit ceiling light.
Dialogue: none.

**3. (5s, 0:07-0:12).** Side-on, the full width of the room, the light in the upper part of the frame. At 0:08 the light clicks off with nobody near it. Mira walks under it, passing at 0:09.5, and stops just beyond it with her back to it.
Mira, at 0:10.5: "One."
Mira, at 0:11.5: "Two."
On "Two" the light clicks on behind her.

**4. (3s, 0:12-0:15).** Mira, close, from the front, the light behind her and out of frame. The lighting is flat. She is not surprised. At 0:13.5 the light clicks off behind her. She does not flinch. She turns around to face it, the back of her shaved head to camera.
Dialogue: none. Sound: the click.

**5. (5s, 0:15-0:20).** Full figure, the camera now on the far side of the light, so she faces us with the light between. She takes three slow steps toward it and stops dead under it at 0:17, the light directly above her in frame, feet together, arms at her sides. She does not walk past it. 0:19 arrives, two seconds on, and nothing happens. Hold.
Dialogue: none. Sound: the room, nothing else.

**6. (4s, 0:20-0:24).** Mira, medium close, the same stillness, the light still off. She keeps her breath shallow. Her eyes stay on the middle distance. Her shoulders come down by a fraction.
Dialogue: none.

**7. (3s, 0:24-0:27).** Close on her right sleeve, the red flight jacket, hanging still at her side. Nothing else in frame. At 0:25.5 the sleeve is pressed in against her arm, as if someone has gone past close to her, then it swings back. Nothing is visible.
Mira, off screen, very quietly, at 0:26.5: "One."

**8. (3s, 0:27-0:30).** Mira, medium close, facing camera, not moving.
Mira, at 0:27.5: "Two."
On "Two" the light clicks on directly above her. She does not look up. Only her eyes move, to her right. Hold to 0:30, hard cut to black.

## Timing

The two-second gap is exact every time:

- Shot 1: passes 0:00.5, click 0:02.5
- Shot 3: passes 0:09.5, click 0:11.5
- Shots 7 and 8: sleeve moves 0:25.5, click 0:27.5

Shot 5 needs the light to stay off through 0:19 and on to the sleeve, so the gap is seen to fail before it holds.

The switch stays on the shelf from shot 2 onward. It is never reconnected.

## Not yet in canon (flagged for continuity, decided by a person, not to be assumed)

1. **Location:** the apartment has a living room with one ceiling light, and a door with a shelf beside it. Canon says only "one apartment".
2. **The two-second delay:** the light comes on two seconds after Mira passes under it, with the switch cut out and the wires capped. Shots 7 and 8 suggest it may not need her to pass, and that stays implied.
3. **The light goes off by itself,** with no fixed time on (5.5s in shots 1 to 3, 2s in shots 3 to 4). This script does not make that a rule.
4. **Her sleeve moves with nothing visible near it.** The episode may imply something unseen in the apartment and never shows or names it. Nothing about what it is, or whether it exists, should be recorded as fact.
5. **Mira's history:** she knew about the light before this episode, cut the switch out herself, and counts the seconds as a habit.
6. **Mira speaks.** Canon sets voice.id but does not say it is hers. This script assumes it is.
7. **The room is visible with the light off.** Canon's lighting is flat, but it names no source. This script shows none and keeps windows out of shot.
