### Shot 1, hook (3s, episode 0:00-0:03)

> Mira, shaved head, red flight jacket. Setting: one apartment. Camera: never leaves the room she is in. Lighting: flat, no lens flare. Format: 9:16 vertical.
>
> Room: a plain living room with pale walls and a bare floor, no furniture near her, no window in shot. One ceiling light, a round white flush fitting. With the light off, everything stays readable at a low, even level with no visible source.
>
> Looking down the length of the room, the ceiling in the top of the frame, framed from the ceiling down to Mira's waist. The very first frame already has this picture, with no fade or lead-in: Mira mid-stride, walking toward camera, directly under the ceiling light, which is off. In her right hand (frame left), held at chest height out in front of the red flight jacket, is a white wall switch on its white faceplate, the faceplate turned square to camera and sharp, two short wires with cut ends hanging from the back of it. The white faceplate reads clearly against the red.
>
> By clip 0.5s she has walked clear of the light and it is behind her. She keeps coming at the same slow pace, looking ahead, and the camera backs away slower than she walks so she grows in the frame, with the light still in frame above and behind her. At clip 2.5s the ceiling light switches on behind her: the whole picture rises evenly by one small step, no beam, no halo, no shadows moving. She does not look back or react. No one else is in the room.

Dialogue: none. Sound: one click at episode 0:02.5.
